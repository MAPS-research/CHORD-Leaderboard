Changelog

[2026-11-22]

TL;DR

The leaderboard now covers prefix continuation, using the results from the ICLR rebuttal of the CHORD paper.

  • New Prefix continuation (DCLM) board: 21 generators continue the same 128-token DCLM-baseline prefixes.
  • Modern open LLMs from the Qwen3.5, Gemma-3, Mistral, Llama-3.1, DCLM and SmolLM2 families are evaluated for the first time.
  • Large diffusion LLMs are added: Dream-7B, LLaDA-8B, SMDM and TESS 2.

Full Changelog

  • Prefix continuation: A second board, chosen with the switch above the type tabs. Each generator continues the same 128-token human prefixes from DCLM-baseline-1.0; continuations are cut to their first 800 GPT-2 tokens and compared with held-out human continuations. Scores are mean±std over 10 non-overlapping sets of 500 prefixes, with the same Qwen3.5-27B CHORD encoder as the unconditional board.
  • New AR generators: Gemma-3-27B-pt, Gemma-3-4B-pt, Mistral-Small-3.1-24B-Base, Ministral-3-3B-Base, Qwen3.5-27B, Qwen3.5-9B-Base, Qwen3.5-4B-Base, Qwen3.5-2B-Base, Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base, Llama-3.1-8B, DCLM-7B, DCLM-1B and SmolLM2-1.7B, alongside GPT-2-large and GPT-2-medium.
  • New diffusion generators: Dream-7B, LLaDA-8B and SMDM (discrete), and TESS 2 (continuous), alongside SEDD-small and MDLM.
  • Open LLMs: The continuation board is not limited to OpenWebText models. The unconditional board still lists only generators trained on OpenWebText.
  • Scores: Taken from the rebuttal table of the CHORD paper (Appendix K). Gemma-3-27B-pt is closest to the human continuations (2.49); within every AR family, larger models are closer.
  • Page: The table notes and evaluation settings follow the selected board. Pending Evaluation is shown with the unconditional board only, and switching boards resets the type tab to All.

[2026-10-08]

TL;DR

Initial release of the CHORD Leaderboard.

  • Open-ended text generators trained on OpenWebText, ranked by CHORD distance to the human reference on unconditional generation.
  • Generators are tagged as AR, discrete diffusion/flow or continuous diffusion/flow, and can be ranked within each type.
  • A weekly search finds new OpenWebText generators, and anyone can request an evaluation.

Full Changelog

  • Launch generators: the six from the CHORD paper (GPT-2-medium, GPT-2-large, MDLM, SEDD-small, LangFlow and ELF-L), then GPT-2-small, GPT-2-XL, ELF-B and ELF-M the same day. The held-out human row scores held-out human text from the same source like a generator.
  • Protocol: 10 non-overlapping sets of 500 unconditional samples of 512 tokens per generator, scored with the 27B CHORD encoder (Qwen3.5-27B) against the paper's human reference. Each generator is evaluated at the setting its paper reports as best.
  • Discovery: Every Monday, new arXiv papers and Hugging Face models are checked for OpenWebText training, public weights and an official repository. Accepted candidates appear under Pending Evaluation until they are scored.
  • Requests: The model evaluation request form asks for a generator to be evaluated, or evaluated sooner.
  • Checkpoints: The bottom of the page lists the exact checkpoint evaluated for each generator.
